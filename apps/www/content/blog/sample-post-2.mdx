# Local Whisper vs. Cloud Models: Why Not Both?

The pitch for local transcription is privacy and latency: Whisper and Parakeet run on your hardware, audio never leaves the machine, and results arrive even on a plane. The pitch for cloud models is breadth: broad language coverage, accents, and background noise handled with more headroom. Most tools make you pick a side. OpenWhispr is built around picking neither — or both.

In OpenWhispr, local models are the default. When a recording genuinely benefits from a cloud model — a noisy meeting room, a language your local model handles poorly — you switch per dictation, from the same hotkey flow. The choice lives in settings, not in a separate app, and your history treats both paths the same.

## Your workflow, your call

Teams can standardize on local-only for sensitive work while individuals opt into cloud models where they help. OpenWhispr Cloud simply keeps the two in sync.
