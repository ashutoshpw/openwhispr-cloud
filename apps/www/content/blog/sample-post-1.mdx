# OpenWhispr Cloud: Your Notes, Synced Everywhere

OpenWhispr started as a desktop dictation app with a simple promise: your voice stays yours. Transcription runs locally with Whisper and Parakeet models, so audio never needs to leave your machine. OpenWhispr Cloud extends that promise instead of replacing it — the desktop experience is unchanged, and the cloud adds the parts that are hard to do alone: sync across devices, shared team workspaces, and programmatic access to everything you have dictated.

Starting today, notes created on your laptop show up on your desktop, and anything you share with a workspace is available to teammates the moment you save it. Teams get invitations, roles, and per-member access, all managed from the dashboard. Privacy controls carry over too: you choose which notes sync and which stay on device.

## What's next

API keys and MCP access are rolling out now, so you can pipe transcripts into scripts, automations, and AI agents. As always, local models remain the default and cloud models remain optional.
